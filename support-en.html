Support

Book Reader organizes and reads your DRM-free PDF, ZIP, CBZ and CBR files.

Import a book

In the Library, tap +, then choose a file in the Files app. You can use a local document, iCloud Drive or an installed file provider.

Your iCloud library is not up to date

Make sure the device is signed in to iCloud and iCloud Drive is enabled. Book Reader keeps books in its private iCloud folder and downloads their contents when needed.

  • Use Verify book files in Settings to locate items that were removed or moved.
  • Use Sync missing covers if some cover images are absent.
  • Keep the app open for a moment when many files need to be analyzed.

A book does not open

  • Make sure it is fully available in iCloud.
  • For CBR, make sure the archive is neither encrypted nor split across volumes.
  • For ZIP or CBZ, make sure the archive contains supported images.
  • EPUB is not supported in this release.

The cover or information is incorrect

Open the book information page. You can edit its fields, choose another page as the cover, or crop the cover to a 4:5 ratio.
